Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it and the choices you have.

Who we are

PCFIC Virtual Assistance Services is a locally owned virtual assistance business based in Tamuning, Guam. This policy covers visits to our website and the services we provide to clients.

Information we collect

  • Contact details — your name, business name, email and WhatsApp number when you check in with us or become a client.
  • Business information — details you share so we can do the work, such as financial records, receipts, payroll details, brand files and social media insights.
  • Account access — page roles or logins you give us to manage your social media, ads or accounting software.
  • Messages — what you send us by WhatsApp, email or our check-in form. Our form does not store your details on our website; it opens WhatsApp with your message ready to send.
  • Basic website data — standard technical information such as browser type and pages visited. We do not use advertising cookies. Our home page shows our Facebook page, and Facebook may set its own cookies under its own policy.

How we use your information

  • To deliver the services you asked for.
  • To send invoices and manage billing, cancellations and refunds.
  • To answer your questions and keep you updated on your work.
  • To tell you about important changes to our prices or policies.

Who we share it with

We never sell or rent your information. We share it only with the tools we need to do our work, such as WhatsApp (messages), Google (email and files), Meta (managing your pages and ads), your accounting software, and Vercel and Supabase (website hosting and database). We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you are a client and for up to two years after our work ends, unless the law requires us to keep records longer (for example, accounting or tax records). You can ask us to delete your information earlier.

How we protect it

Our website uses HTTPS, our database is access-protected, and your financial data and account access are handled only by our team. When our work ends, we ask you to remove our access to your pages and accounts, and we stop using any logins you shared.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or stop receiving messages from us, at any time by contacting us by WhatsApp or email.

Children

Our services are for businesses and adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with a new “last updated” date.
