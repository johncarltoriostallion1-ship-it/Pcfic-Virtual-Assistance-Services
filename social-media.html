Common questions

Which platforms do you manage?

Mainly Facebook and Instagram, plus TikTok and LinkedIn when they fit your audience.

Do I get to approve posts first?

Yes. You'll see each month's content before anything goes live.

Is the ad budget included?

Our fee covers managing the ads. The ad spend itself is paid directly to the platform, so you stay in control of it.

How many posts do I get per month?

It depends on the plan. Message us and we'll recommend a posting schedule that fits your goals and budget.
