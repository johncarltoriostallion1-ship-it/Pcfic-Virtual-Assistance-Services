Terms & ConditionsPrivacy PolicyRefund Policy

We want you to be happy with our work. Here is how cancellations and refunds work.

Summary

  • Monthly services are billed monthly. Cancel any time.
  • No charges after your cancellation is confirmed.
  • Full refund within 7 days of a payment if we have not started that month's work or project.
  • Duplicate or mistaken charges are always refunded in full.

Canceling a monthly service

Let us know by WhatsApp or email at any time. Your service continues until the end of the current billing month, and you will not be charged again after your cancellation is confirmed.

Refund eligibility

  • Within 7 days of a payment — if we have not yet started the work for that month or project, you can request a full refund of that payment.
  • Work already started or delivered — payments for work in progress or already delivered are not refundable. You keep access to your service until the end of the paid month.
  • One-time projects — deposits are refundable within 7 days if work has not started. Once design or development has begun, the deposit covers the work done.
  • Ad spend — money paid directly to advertising platforms is not paid to us and cannot be refunded by us.
  • Duplicate or mistaken charges — always refunded in full, whenever you report them.

How to request a refund

Send us a message by WhatsApp or email with your full name, business name and the date of the payment. We reply within one business day.

When you receive your refund

Refunds go back to the original payment method. Depending on your bank or payment provider, it can take 5 to 10 business days to appear.

Billing questions

If you see a charge you don't recognise, please contact us first so we can sort it out quickly, before opening a dispute with your bank.
