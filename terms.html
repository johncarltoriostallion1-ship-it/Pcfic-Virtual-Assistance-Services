1. Who we are

PCFIC Virtual Assistance Services (“PCFIC”, “we”, “us”) is a locally owned virtual assistance business based in Tamuning, Guam. We provide social media, accounting and web & creative services to businesses.

By booking or paying for any of our services, you agree to these Terms, our Refund Policy and our Privacy Policy.

2. Our services

We offer social media management (content, design, copywriting, page management, ads and reports), accounting support (bookkeeping, payroll, financial statements and tax-ready records) and web & creative work (websites, logo design and video editing).

The exact services, deliverables and schedule for your business are set out in the proposal or quote we agree with you before work begins. All work is delivered digitally — through WhatsApp, email, shared folders or the platforms we manage for you.

3. Pricing and billing

Monthly services are billed as a fixed monthly fee, as stated in your proposal. There is no long-term contract unless your proposal says otherwise.

One-time projects (such as a website, logo or video) are quoted in advance. We may ask for a deposit before starting, with the balance due on delivery.

Payment is due on the dates shown on your invoice. If a payment is late, we may pause work until it is settled.

4. Getting started

After you accept a proposal, we schedule a short onboarding call to learn about your business, goals and current setup, and to collect the access and files we need. We normally reach out within one business day.

5. Canceling a monthly service

You can cancel a monthly service at any time by letting us know by WhatsApp or email. Your service stays active until the end of the current billing month, and you will not be charged for any month after your cancellation is confirmed.

6. Refunds

Refunds are handled under our Refund Policy, which forms part of these Terms.

7. Advertising budgets

Our fee covers managing your ads. The ad spend itself is paid directly by you to the platform (such as Meta). We do not control platform charges and cannot refund them.

8. Your responsibilities

Please give us accurate information, timely approvals and the access we need (for example, page roles or accounting records). You confirm that any logos, photos and content you send us are yours to use. Delays in approvals or access may delay delivery.

9. Ownership of the work

Once a project is paid in full, you own the final designs, videos and website content we create for you. We may show finished work in our portfolio unless you ask us not to. Third-party items (such as fonts, stock images, software or platform accounts) remain subject to their own licences.

10. Confidentiality

We keep your financial records, business information and account access private. We only use them to deliver your services and never sell or share them, except as described in our Privacy Policy.
